Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Direct Off Market LLC ("DOM," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when using this investor portal. By creating an account or using this platform, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password when you register
  • Investor profile: estimated liquidity, investment criteria (buy box), and investment strategies you provide
  • Transaction records: offers you submit, deals you inquire about, and saved pro forma analyses
  • Contact form submissions: messages, questions, and support requests you send us
  • Usage data: pages you visit, listings you view, and actions you take on the portal

We may also receive limited technical data automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information when you access the portal.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide access to off-market deal listings and portal features
  • Match your investor criteria (buy box) with relevant properties
  • Process offers and expressions of interest you submit
  • Send you deal alerts, property notifications, and portal updates (you may opt out at any time)
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Improve the portal experience and understand how investors use the platform
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the portal. Each has its own privacy policy governing its use of your data:

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure. Your account credentials and profile data are stored securely in Supabase's cloud environment.
  • Mapbox — interactive property maps displayed on the marketplace and deal pages. Mapbox may log tile requests associated with your session.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage analytics to help us understand portal performance and improve the user experience. Google Analytics does not receive your name or contact information.
  • Netlify — web hosting and serverless function infrastructure.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) for all data in transit. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no internet transmission is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials confidential.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and investor data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide services. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as transaction records.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information in your profile
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@directoffmarket.com.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The portal uses browser localStorage to save your theme preference (dark/light mode) and your saved favorites list. No cross-site tracking cookies are placed by DOM. Third-party services (Mapbox, Google Analytics) may set cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

This portal is intended for adults (18+) who are sophisticated investors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email or through the portal. Continued use of the portal after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised.
